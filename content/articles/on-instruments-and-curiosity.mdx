A good instrument does not do the thinking for you. It makes a thought **visible enough to be argued with**. That is the whole idea behind this site: treat tools, notes and sketches as ways of looking harder.

## Why start with a notebook

Notebooks are forgiving. A half-formed sketch can sit beside a finished diagram without apology, and the reader can see how one became the other.

<PullQuote>Sketches are arguments you are not yet committed to.</PullQuote>

When an idea survives being drawn three different ways, it usually deserves a prototype.

## A small comparison

| Medium | Good for | Weak at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sketch | Finding the shape of an idea | Precision |
| Prototype | Testing a belief | Longevity |
| Essay | Defending a position | Speed |
